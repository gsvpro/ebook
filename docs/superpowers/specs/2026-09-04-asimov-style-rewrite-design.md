# Asimov Style Rewrite — Design

Date: 2026-09-04

## Goal

Put the English Lars Odin book back into the author’s Swedish voice: long packed sentences, ideas carried in the paragraph, dry infrequent humor, science spoken in-scene the way a tired engineer talks at a whiteboard. Later English AI passes turned this into sitcom punches and clever diagnoses. That is not the target.

## Style source

Primary: `Lars_Odin_sci-fi copy.pdf` (extracted working copy: `_swedish_source.txt`).

That Swedish is the voice lock. The approved English sample is a close translation of its opening, not a literary polish of `Lars_Odin_English_Manuscript.md`.

Do not use as style sources:

- the old character-voice bible (Lars “short punches”). Replaced 2026-09-04 in `docs/character-voices.md`.
- the “lighten manuscript readability for beta readers” pass
- sitcom dialogue from earlier preferred-merged voice-bible passes

Use as style sources:

- Swedish PDF / `_swedish_source.txt`
- approved Chapters 1–2
- the rewritten `docs/character-voices.md`

## Voice lock

- Sentences may run. A paragraph can hold payment, shock, temptation, and a grimace without becoming a joke stack.
- Humor is ordinary and scarce: “that would be sinking a little too low,” food, work, irritation. Not sitcom, not punchline-every-exchange.
- Science is explained as speech or work: napkin, blackboard, “I read a lot.” Do not name the problem in a clever diagnosis.
- Avoid “X is not Y. It is Z.” as a tic.
- Mystery stays mystery. Early Lars does not know he is Aerit.
- No slogan prose. No stacks of one-word paragraphs.
- Do not invent nicknames, product lectures, or jokes that are not in the Swedish or required by later plot.

## Method

1. Translate PDF-backed scenes closely (Chapter 1 done as sample; rest of PDF-backed early book next).
2. For English-only chapters (most of 2–16 expansion, all of 17–55): keep events, rewrite the English into this voice.
3. Fold known canon/timeline fixes when a chapter is touched.
