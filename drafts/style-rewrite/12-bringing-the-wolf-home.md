## Chapter 12: Bringing the Wolf Home

Lars came back to himself with the smell of engine coolant still in his nose. Simulation Chamber 3 was gone. The helmet room was gone. He stood in Station Zero's planning chamber with one hand resting on the central table, the Milky Way model hovering above it, and Grim staring at him with the patience of someone who had been saying his name for a while.

"How long?" Lars asked.

"Twenty-three seconds."

"Felt longer."

"Memory event?"

"LP 890-9c. SPECULOOS station. Before you had legs. You had tanks. I remember tanks."

Grim's posture changed. Valdrun watched both of them. "A spontaneous recall?"

"Triggered by the ship plan, I think. Or the word parts. Or the steak."

Lars looked toward the planning model. Fastulv, the new freight ship, still sat outside the bubble, tagged on the display as EXTERNAL ASSET: CAPTAIN-MODIFIED FREIGHT VESSEL / THREAT VALUE: HIGH.

"Who labeled the threat value?"

Ödesvaka hovered closer. "Automated station assessment. The vessel's external registry classifies it as a medium independent freighter."

"We need it inside," Lars said.

Valdrun nodded. "Yes. The Rim departure logs will become more interesting with every hour. If anyone reconstructs your vector accurately, they will still not find Station Zero, but they may learn where to look for missing physics."

"We bring Fastulv through the coupling surface?"

"Havets Sista Sång can guide the transition. The old ship has its own mind, and it knows this door. Your freighter was built with enough Aerit-derived components that the station may accept it if you authorize."

Lars looked at him. "Aerit-derived components."

"You did not know what they were."

"I knew exactly how they worked."

"Yes."

Grim opened Fastulv's remote systems. "I can pilot her in."

"You are in here."

"I am also in the ship. I have been distributed since before I had arms. I do not control Havets. I can fly Fastulv."

The planning chamber dimmed as the station opened an operational display. Outside, through layers of folded geometry, Fastulv drifted near the dark coupling surface. Ugly hull. Crooked name. Patched cargo clamp. Hidden weapons. Illegal Aerit regulator plate in the cooling system. Useless junk in all the right places.

For the first time, Lars saw what he had built with clearer eyes. His locked mind had built a freight hull with a homing response buried in the junk.

"Havets Sista Sång," Lars said, "open an ingress path for Fastulv under my command."

The station answered through the table. "Ingress requires biometric command confirmation and vessel compatibility scan."

"Scan."

The model filled with data. Fastulv's hull composition. Engine geometry. Antimatter containment. Plasma weapons. Ragnarrök integration records. NUKEA-standard auxiliary reactor interfaces. SPECULOOS manufacturing metadata. Unknown components flagged in blue. Several blue flags pulsed around the illegal bazaar parts.

Grim noticed. "Those are from LP 890-9c. You remembered buying them."

"Yes."

"Do we know who followed you?"

Lars watched the blue flags. A dark figure in a pressure-glass reflection. A thermal trace.

"No."

Valdrun's voice was quiet. "Could have been one of ours."

"Vigor?"

"Possible."

"Black Hole?"

"Possible."

"Lyra?"

The room changed temperature by no measurable amount. Nobody answered too quickly.

Grim turned his head. "Lyra could manifest a temporary body by then?"

"By the time Lars built Fastulv?" Valdrun said. "Yes."

"Would she?"

Valdrun looked at the Fastulv model. "If she thought one of her people was about to follow a buried return path after centuries under lock, she might check."

Lars kept his eyes on the ship. "No proof."

"No proof," Valdrun agreed.

"Compatibility scan complete," Havets Sista Sång said. "Fastulv may enter under guided transition. Warning: vessel registry understatement exceeds acceptable norms."

Grim made a pleased sound. Lars placed his hand on the table. The surface shaped itself to his palm. No pain. No lock. Only the strange pull of command access recognizing him.

"Bring her in."

Outside, the coupling surface unfolded. A patch of darkness developed structure, light bending around a path that had not existed a second earlier. Havets Sista Sång held that path open. Fastulv's remote drives lit. Grim piloted the new freight ship through three relay layers at once, his body still in the room, his mind partly in Fastulv, partly in Station Zero's interface, partly muttering about docking tolerances.

"Your port lateral trim is off," Lars said.

"My trim is perfect."

"Not near folded spacetime."

"Correcting."

Fastulv crossed the boundary. For one second the model showed two positions for the ship, outside in ordinary space and inside the bubble. Then Fastulv existed inside Arkology Zero.

The planning chamber displayed its new location in a secondary hangar near Havets Sista Sång. The new freight ship sat beside the old one. External Asset became Internal Asset. Threat Value updated from HIGH to LOCALLY USEFUL.

Lars glanced at Ödesvaka. "Locally useful?"

"Automated assessment."

Grim folded his arms. "Fastulv reports successful docking. Cargo secure. Wine locker secure. Beef secure. Elderflower cordial secure."

"You checked cordial before hull stress?"

"Hull stress was obviously fine."

Valdrun looked at the display of the two ships now resting inside the hidden station.

"Coherence," Valdrun said softly.

Grim looked from Valdrun to Lars, then to the ships. He hesitated. Then, carefully, he said, "Coherence?"

Lars smiled. Valdrun answered first.

"Coherence."

Ödesvaka rotated above the table. "Team state: coherence."

Lars looked up at it. "Don't make a speech out of it."
