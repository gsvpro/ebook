## Chapter 14: Havets Sista Sång

Havets Sista Sång did not have a bridge chair, and this annoyed Lars immediately.

"Where am I supposed to sit?"

The old ship's command deck lit around him in soft bands of white and pale blue. The central console had unfolded into a waist-high crescent, and surrounding stations waited in standby, each one clean enough to make Aphex laboratories look like budget kitchens. The ship said the command interface was ambulatory and that the captain had traditionally stood during active transitions. Lars said the captain traditionally had better knees, and the ship answered that his knees were within acceptable tolerance. Grim stood at the secondary interface and made a small sound that Lars chose not to encourage.

Valdrun entered behind them with a compact sidearm, a dark utility harness, and the look of someone stepping into an old house after a long absence. He stopped just inside the command deck. For a moment the blue-white light touched his face and the centuries showed.

"Coherence?" Lars asked quietly.

Valdrun blinked, then nodded.

"Coherence."

Grim watched, absorbing. The ship spoke. Guardian Valdrun recognized. Command access restored under Captain Laurðinn's active envelope. Welcome back. Valdrun exhaled once through his nose and said it was still formal. The ship said he had preferred formal during last deployment. Valdrun said he had been younger then.

The command display expanded into a field of navigable states. The interior of Arkology Zero appeared as geometry nested inside geometry, then the outer coupling surface, then ordinary space beyond, then the estimated relay location. Havets Sista Sång did not plot a path the way Fastulv did. Fastulv was a freight ship. It flew from here to there. The old ship calculated permissions: which layer of spacetime could be touched, which curve could be leaned on, which external observer would see nothing, or something boring, or the wrong thing entirely.

Lars put his hand on the console. The ship recognized him down to blood, neural phase, and the old command habits still unfolding inside him. No pain, no hesitation.

"Status," Lars said.

"Primary drive nominal. Fold-surface coupling nominal. Local stealth envelope nominal. Weapons restricted under Alpha Fourteen unless command override and guardian concurrence."

"Weapons restricted?"

Valdrun said yes. Lars disliked discovering adult supervision in his own ship. Valdrun said Lars had written part of the restriction, and that he had been occasionally sensible.

Grim opened the weapons layer and froze.

"Lars. This vessel could cut Fastulv apart without using its main battery."

Havets Sista Sång responded that Fastulv was classified as a locally useful allied vessel and that cutting was not recommended, subject to behavior.

Then the ship moved. The deck did not lurch, there was no engine roar and no dramatic shudder, the galaxy on the display simply changed relationship to them. Arkology Zero released Havets Sista Sång through an internal fold, and the old ship slid from hidden space into the ragged edge of the KBC Void.

External cameras showed nothing the instruments could hold. The ship bent observation around itself so completely that even its own display had to draw a symbolic hull outline for the crew. Anyone watching from The Rim would have seen empty dust.

The relay target lay ahead, a derelict ice hauler registered two centuries ago, dead engines, hollow cargo spine, tumbling slowly near a debris ribbon. It looked abandoned. Havets highlighted four passive sensor petals folded into the hauler's shadow, a neutrino tick too regular for background, and a microburst recorder that had caught Fastulv's vector correction from The Rim.

"Black Hole?" Lars asked.

Grim analyzed. Shell hardware, not branded, methods aligned. It was listening for impossible departures. Yes, they could erase it. Valdrun stepped closer and asked if they could learn from it first.

Havets Sista Sång did not fire a gun. There was nothing to see, no beam, no flash. The old ship sent a thin probe of controlled matter across the vacuum without thrust. The probe touched the derelict hauler and spoke to the relay's outer shell the way a maintenance crew would. The shell believed maintenance had arrived. The relay opened.

Grim leaned in. Data streamed into the command field, not enough to compromise Arkology Zero, but enough to show Black Hole B-7 had expected Lars to run. Not to where. Just away. They had seeded passive ears along several routes, and one route labeled statistically Swedish.

Lars stared.

"Statistically Swedish?"

Grim said his public persona had a pattern. Valdrun's mouth twitched.

The relay contained a partial packet meant for a courier drone: Fastulv vector anomaly, Aphex engagement, possible enhanced target survival, priority escalation to acquisition cell, request for blood-compatible containment.

The room went quiet. Lars read the last line twice.

"Blood-compatible containment."

Valdrun's face hardened. Grim's voice went flat. They had come prepared.

Havets Sista Sång waited.

"Copy the relay," Lars said. "Plant the false trail to the wellness moon. Make it look like Fastulv's drive wake clipped the hauler by accident and corrupted the buffer."

Grim worked with the ship. Valdrun watched the external model and said they should leave them a reason not to trust the data too much. Agreed. Lars added a signature smear, a hint of Aphex military encryption, a NUKEA reactor diagnostic ghost, and a corrupted influencer-era advertising packet for his old channel, including a defunct grooming product sponsorship. Grim paused over that last piece, then put it in.

The relay accepted the corrupted record. Then Havets Sista Sång killed it. There was still no blast and no heat. The old ship reached into the relay's memory. The programs that were supposed to keep the petals open and the recorder running began to give opposite orders. The petals curled inward. The recorder cracked without a sound. The circuits inside went to dust. The ice hauler kept tumbling. From far away, nothing had happened.

"Quiet Dignity complete," Grim said.

Lars looked at Valdrun.

"Too easy."

Valdrun nodded. Yes.

The command deck lights shifted amber. Havets Sista Sång spoke. Incoming object, low thermal, high acceleration, masked approach from debris shadow. Grim snapped the tactical layer open. A black dart emerged from the hauler's broken cargo spine, too fast and too cold to be accidental.

"Trap," Valdrun said.

"Now it is a field test."
