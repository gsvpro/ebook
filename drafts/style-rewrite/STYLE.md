# Voice lock for this rewrite

Read `01-the-rim.md` first. That is the approved English. It is a close translation of the author's Swedish PDF, not a literary polish.

## Do this

- Long packed sentences. A paragraph can hold several actions.
- Informal spoken English, slightly messy, like translated Swedish.
- Humor is ordinary and scarce: food, work, irritation, "sinking a little too low."
- Science as talk at a board or work with hands. "I read a lot." Napkin. Not a diagnosis.
- Keep every plot event, name, and canon fact in the chapter you are rewriting.
- Replace the whole file. Do not leave old sitcom lines sitting next to new ones.

## Do not do this

- Sitcom punchlines, banter tennis, one-liners every exchange.
- "X is not Y. It is Z." as a habit.
- Clever machine/people diagnoses ("obeying the wrong part of its own control stack").
- One-word paragraph stacks.
- Invented jokes, nicknames, or lectures not required by the scene.
- Narrator winks: "proper," "the places the sensors always missed," "someone who did not care about the joins."
- No stacks of five-word combat sentences. If Havets interferes with a probe, say that it got into the steering, that nothing visible fired, and then what the ship did. The reader has to be able to follow it.

## Clarity (required)

If a smart reader who has not studied the worldbuilding cannot picture the action, the sentence has failed.

- Name the actor and the thing they do. Fastulv is the visible new freight ship. Havets is the hidden old ship. Say so when it matters.
- Science is a person explaining in the room: what they see, what it will do, what they need. Not a label.
- Combat is a sequence of visible events, in order, in ordinary words. Not "the probe's loops stopped agreeing."
- Show the hands. If Mira fakes damage, she sits at the console, works the heat on a brace, marks a crack on the stress plot, and loads the metal so the numbers match. Do not write "she put the false heat onto the ring."
- Do not wink. "The station groaned where the lie had to look real" is the narrator scoring the scene. Say the ring creaked because she loaded the brace.
- Short sentences are for shock. The rest should be long packed sentences like Chapter 1, with the how inside them. Chopping a skipped action into five short sentences is not clarity.
