# Chapter 53: Coherence Breaks

Ren Koss died four days after First Door. 

His body had flown a route no living pilot should have known, under field stress, after Black Hole trauma and emergency acceleration. The medical diagnostic was absolute: cascading vascular failure. His nanobots, pushed beyond their rebuild threshold, were failing to stabilize the tissue.

The recovery suite on Station Null was a study in monochromatic silence: brushed-titanium bulkheads that absorbed sound, and soft, indirect light that seemed to emanate from the architecture itself. There were no flickering screens or clunky consoles; only seamless glass interfaces that integrated into the walls, showing vitals as rhythmic, pulsing light—the only evidence that Ren was still anchored to the station’s grid.

"Station Null has the clean backup," Mira said from the remote channel, her projection a high-fidelity render that held perfect presence against the wall. "I'm initiating the resynthesis now."

Lars leaned against a seamless bulkhead, his gaze fixed on the diagnostic flow. "Hold the write. He’s in a metastable coherence-dip."

Mira’s projection stilled. "A dip?"

"The KBC-anomalin didn't just stress his body; it shifted his neural signature," Lars explained, his voice steady. "He's currently distributed across a phase-shifted coherence structure. If we force the resynthesis now, the backup will clash with the residual resonance in his substrate. We'll fracture the consciousnessal stream."

"It takes weeks for a phase-transition to resolve," Mira noted, her voice flat.

"Then we wait," Lars said. "We don't do 'partial' and we don't do 'fast' when it comes to the stack. We keep him in quarantine until the resonance collapses back into a single state. The integrity of the pilot is the integrity of the mission."

Ren laughed, a wet, rattling sound that broke the clinical silence. He looked at Lars, then at the seamless, white walls of the suite. "Stop making my blood sound like it lost a committee vote."

"It lost a pressure gradient, Ren," Mira insisted, her voice softening.

"See?" Ren grinned weakly at Lars. "She respects me enough to insult the plumbing."
